# MAST Community Dataset Suggestion — Submission #3

## Dataset Name
OGLE–KMTNet Unified High‑Cadence Microlensing Light Curve Catalog for Roman Black Hole and Exoplanet Discovery

---

## Dataset Summary, Scientific Impact, and Use Case

This submission proposes that the Mikulski Archive for Space Telescopes (MAST) host a unified, science‑ready catalog of microlensing light curves combining data from the Optical Gravitational Lensing Experiment (OGLE) and the Korea Microlensing Telescope Network (KMTNet). Together, these surveys provide complementary temporal coverage of the Galactic bulge: OGLE offers long‑baseline, uniform monitoring, while KMTNet delivers continuous, high‑cadence observations from three longitudinally separated observatories.

While both datasets are publicly available, they are currently hosted separately, use different formats, and require significant effort to combine for scientific analysis. This fragmentation limits their effectiveness as precursor datasets for the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope. In particular, Roman’s Galactic Bulge Time‑Domain Survey (GBTDS) will rely on historical light‑curve context to interpret astrometric microlensing signals, identify planetary anomalies, and confirm long‑timescale events associated with isolated black holes and neutron stars.

By ingesting a unified OGLE–KMTNet light‑curve catalog as a High‑Level Science Product (HLSP), MAST would provide the community with discovery‑ready infrastructure that enables immediate Roman co‑analysis. This dataset transforms legacy ground‑based monitoring into a powerful foundation for Roman’s time‑domain science, ensuring that Roman observations are never analyzed in isolation from their historical context.

---

## Why This Dataset Should Be Ingested at MAST

MAST is the designated archive for Roman science products. Co‑locating precursor ground‑based microlensing light curves within the Roman analysis ecosystem minimizes duplication of effort across the community and enables seamless joint modeling workflows. Hosting this dataset at MAST ensures broad accessibility, consistent metadata standards, and efficient interoperability with Roman data products.

## Dataset Type and Structure

- **Primary data type:** Time‑series photometric light curves  
- **Secondary products:** Event‑level summary catalog  
- **Format:** CSV or database tables compliant with Roman metadata standards  
- **Scope:** Long‑timescale and planetary‑sensitive microlensing events

---

## Roman Surveys and Science Cases Enabled

- **Primary Roman overlap:** Galactic Bulge Time‑Domain Survey (GBTDS)

**Science cases enabled:**
- Confirmation of isolated black holes and neutron stars via astrometric microlensing
- Detection and characterization of wide‑orbit exoplanets
- Microlensing population statistics and anomaly studies
- Time‑domain astrophysics in dense stellar fields

- **Cost profile:** Moderate but justified by unique Roman leverage
- **Status:** Public, non‑Roman, static legacy survey data
